# Problem Statement

## Users
- **Guests**: travelers who want to find and book a hotel room.
- **Hotel staff**: people who manage rooms and view bookings.

## Goal
Guests want to quickly find an available room in a city for their dates, compare prices, and book it.
Staff want to see which rooms are booked and avoid double bookings.

## Problem
Booking rooms by hand (paper, spreadsheets, messages) leads to double bookings, wrong prices,
and lost data. This application automates searching, pricing, and booking, and saves all data
between runs.
